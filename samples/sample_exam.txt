B. Transpiration
C. Condensation
D. Precipitation

Q4. What type of bond is formed when electrons are shared between atoms?
A. Ionic bond
B. Covalent bond
C. Hydrogen bond
D. Metallic bond

Q5. Which organelle is responsible for packaging and shipping proteins within a cell?
A. Ribosome
B. Endoplasmic reticulum
C. Golgi apparatus
D. Lysosome

Answer Key

Q1 "B" Energy production through ATP (Page 45)
